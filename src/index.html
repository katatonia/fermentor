About us

Born in Saint‑Petersburg in 2018, we’re a contract brewery brewing on the historic Arsenal grounds.

With the freedom to experiment and produce batches starting from 200 liters, we’ve built a brand recognized worldwide and highly rated across the industry.

We make bold craft beers across the spectrum — from clean lagers and classic ales to rich stouts, hop-forward IPAs, and experimental gose.

We also craft meads, ciders, and non-alcoholic drinks — and we’re ready to bring your boldest ideas and brewing experiments to life.

Our experienced brewers and state-of-the-art equipment ensure the highest quality, whether we’re working with your recipes or developing them together.
