Founded in St. Petersburg in 2018, we’re a contract brewery located on the grounds of the Arsenal plant.

With the freedom to experiment and produce batches starting from 200 liters, we’ve built a brand recognized worldwide and highly rated across the industry.

We make bold craft beers across the spectrum — from clean lagers and classic ales to rich stouts, hop-forward IPAs, and experimental gose.

We also craft meads, ciders, and non-alcoholic drinks — and we’re ready to bring your boldest ideas and brewing experiments to life.

Our experienced brewers and state-of-the-art equipment ensure the highest quality, whether we’re working with your recipes or developing them together.

How do I buy tickets for a tour?

You can book a convenient date and time and pay for the tour on our website. Click here

What's included in a tour?
Tour itinerary:
  • Welcome drink and snacks at the Hop Hunting Club bar
  • Introduction to brewing equipment and the distillation process in the showroom «Nevskaya Vinokurnya»
  • Tour of the ferMENTOR brewery and an introduction to all stages of beer production, from malt to bottling line
  • Visit to one of the largest cold rooms in Russia
  • Rare facts and myth-busting about your favorite drink from our guide
  • Tasting of 5 beers during the tour, including the opportunity to try beer straight from the tank
How do I purchase a gift certificate for a tour or masterclass?

You can purchase physical gift certificates for tours at our Hop Hunting Club bar.

Is there an age limit for participating in a tour or masterclass?

All visitors to the site must be 18 years of age or older.

Is there a limit on the number of participants in a masterclass group?
  • A group masterclass can accommodate up to 5 people; the price is per person
  • An individual masterclass is designed for one person, but you can invite one friend (this is included in the price)
  • «A Day with a Brewer» is limited to one participant
Is there a limit on the number of participants in a tour group?

Tour groups can range from 2 to 12 people. If you would like an exclusive tour without outsiders, please notify us in advance by phone: +7 981 762 47 43

What kind of beer does fermentor brew?
