Is there an age limit for participating in the tour?

All visitors to the site must be 18 years of age or older.

Is there a limit on the number of participants in a group?

Tour groups can range from 2 to 12 people. If you would like an exclusive tour without outsiders, please notify us in advance by phone: +7 981 762 47 43

What's included in the tour?
Tour itinerary:
  • Welcome drink and snacks at the Hop Hunting Club bar
  • Introduction to brewing equipment and the distillation process in the showroom «Nevskaya Vinokurnya»
  • Tour of the ferMENTOR brewery and an introduction to all stages of beer production, from malt to bottling line
  • Visit to one of the largest cold rooms in Russia
  • Rare facts and myth-busting about your favorite drink from our guide
  • Tasting of 5 beers during the tour, including the opportunity to try beer straight from the tank
